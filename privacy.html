Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Bloombyte Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Pixelated BloomByte to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and
  • read the name of the signed-in channel, to make sure uploads go to the right channel.

How the data is used and stored

The app stores its Google sign-in token only on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
